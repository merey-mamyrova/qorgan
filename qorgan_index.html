Ask for help without taking out your phone.

Qorgan is a personal-safety system made of a mobile app and a discreet wearable. Hold the button for two seconds and your location and an audio recording go to the people you trust.

The problem

When it matters, a phone is too slow.

840 million women have experienced partner or sexual violence in their lifetime. World Health Organization, November 2025.

Asking for help takes steps

Unlock the phone, open an app, dial. Each step costs time you may not have.

Reaching for a phone can make things worse

In a tense moment, visible action can escalate the situation.

The people who can help are often nearby

Friends and family may be far away. Someone a few hundred meters off can arrive first.

How it works

Three moves, no screen needed.

Step 1

Hold

Press the wearable's button for two seconds. It vibrates once the app confirms the alert.

Step 2

Alert

The app shares your live location and starts recording audio. Your trusted contacts get a message in Telegram. They do not need the app.

Step 3

Nearby help

Qorgan users within 500 meters get a nearby-SOS alert. Partner venues, such as cafes and pharmacies with trained staff, are marked as places to go.

What is inside

An app and a wearable that back each other up.

Safety timer

Set a timer for a walk home. If you do not confirm you are safe, your contacts are alerted.

Fake call

A call that sounds like someone you know, using the real voices of your loved ones, so you can leave an uncomfortable situation.

Partner map

Cafes, pharmacies and shops with trained staff, shown on the map as places you can walk into.

Pulse sensor

The wearable reads your heart rate and streams it to the app over Bluetooth.

Low-battery alert

If your phone is about to die, your contacts are told, so a silent phone is never a surprise.

Three languages

The full interface is available in English, Russian and Kazakh.
